Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. This policy explains how Captain B LLC ("Captain B", "we") handles the information you share on this website.

What we collect

When you ask for an estimate, we collect the details you type into the form: your name, phone number, project ZIP code, and, if you choose, your email address, timeline and project notes. When you call us, your phone carrier shares your number with us.

How we use it

We use your details to contact you about your project, prepare your estimate and answer your questions. We do not sell your information.

Website measurement

This website may use tools such as Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our advertising. These tools can use cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the website, receive form requests and contact customers, and only for those purposes, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

To ask what information we have about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, call (407) 881-7910.
