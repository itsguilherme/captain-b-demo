What types of projects does Captain B handle?

Screen and pool enclosures, custom lanais, pergolas and outdoor structures, paver installation, artificial turf, custom outdoor kitchens, decorative waterfalls and water features, and outdoor living renovations, for homes and commercial properties.

Do you serve areas outside Orlando?

Yes. Captain B serves Orlando and the surrounding areas of Central Florida. Share your ZIP code and the team will confirm your area.

Can my project be customized?

Yes. Every property has different dimensions, needs and goals, so each project is planned around your space and what you want from it.

Can I combine several services in one project?

Yes. Enclosures, pavers, pergolas, outdoor kitchens, turf and other elements can be planned together as one outdoor project.

Do you handle commercial projects?

Yes. Captain B works on residential and commercial outdoor projects.
